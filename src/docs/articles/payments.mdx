# Payments

Plans control festival limits and advanced features.

## Plan impact

- Basic is for smaller festivals and single-owner use.
- Standard adds larger operational workflows.
- Pro unlocks larger scale, institutional workflows, and custom subdomains.

## Upgrades

Use billing or plan screens to upgrade when you reach limits or need features such as custom domains.
