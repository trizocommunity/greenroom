# Participants

Participants are the people or teams competing in festival programmes.

## Common tasks

1. Add participants manually or through bulk upload.
2. Assign each participant to a group.
3. Place participants into categories.
4. Assign participants to programmes.
5. Share participant login links when the event is ready.

## Participant login

Participant login gives participants a focused view of their programmes, schedules, group details, and festival updates.
