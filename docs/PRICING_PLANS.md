## 💰 Pricing Plans

**Duration:** 30 Days  
**Best For:** Small local festivals, schools, and first-time organizers

## 🚀 Getting Started

### How to Choose Your Plan

**Choose BASIC if:**
- You're organizing a small local festival (under 250 participants)
- You need basic management features
- Budget is a primary concern
- You're testing the platform for the first time

**Choose STANDARD if:** ⭐ **Recommended**
- You're organizing a medium-sized festival (250-500 participants)
- You need advanced features like certificates and analytics
- You want better support response times
- You need team collaboration features

**Choose PRO if:**
- You're organizing a large-scale festival (500+ participants)
- You need unlimited programmes and stages
- You require dedicated support and account management
- You want API access and custom integrations

## 💳 Payment & Activation

### Payment Methods
- UPI
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Net Banking
- Razorpay

### Activation Process
1. Select your desired plan
2. Complete payment via Razorpay
3. Festival unlocked instantly
4. Start managing your festival immediately

### Post-Expiry Access

**BASIC Plan:**  
⚠️ All festival data is **permanently deleted** upon expiration (30 days)  
⚠️ No read-only access - data cannot be recovered  
⚠️ Export your data before expiration  
⚠️ Must purchase a new festival for future events  

**STANDARD & PRO Plans:**  
✓ **Read-only access** after expiration  
✓ Your data is never deleted  
✓ You can upgrade or renew anytime  
✓ Export all data before or after expiration
